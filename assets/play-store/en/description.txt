FULL DESCRIPTION (4000 chars max)
sis is a self-hosted listening tracker. A sis server, running on your own machine, records every song you play on Spotify and keeps your whole history in a database you control. This app is its client: it connects to your server and shows you everything it has logged.

What you need
• A sis server. It's free, its source code is public and it runs with Docker; the step-by-step guide is at sis.fm/guide.
• Or an invite to someone else's server. sis.fm, the developer's own instance, is invite-only.
• A Spotify account. Servers that enable it also let you sign in with Last.fm.

On first launch the app asks for your server's address, and you can switch servers at any time from Settings.

What it shows
• Now playing — your current track, live
• History — every play, grouped by listening session
• Rankings — top tracks, albums and artists by play count or listening time, over any range
• Weekly charts — new entries, re-entries, peaks and weeks on chart
• Reports — weekly, monthly and yearly recaps of your top artists, albums and tracks
• Insights — hour-by-day heatmaps, genres, streaks and discovery
• Concerts — log the shows you've seen, with their setlists
• Import and export — bring in your Spotify data download, export everything as JSON or CSV

Built for the way you listen
Switch any ranking between plays and time. Filter by all-time, year, 6 months, 3 months, month, week, day or a custom range. Jump to any track, artist or album for its own detail page and history.

Fast and focused
A dense, monospace dashboard with charts that load instantly, keyboard shortcuts for power users, and a global search to find anything in seconds.

Your data, on your server
Your whole listening history stays in a database you control: no third-party analytics, no selling your taste back to you. You can delete your account and all its data from Settings at any time.

sis is an independent project and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Spotify.
