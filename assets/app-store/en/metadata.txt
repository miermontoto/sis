FULL DESCRIPTION (4000 chars max)
sis is a self-hosted listening tracker. A sis server, running on your own machine, records every song you play on Spotify and keeps your whole history in a database you control. This app is its client: it connects to your server and shows you everything it has logged.

What you need
• A sis server. It's free, its source code is public and it runs with Docker; the step-by-step guide is at sis.fm/guide.
• Or an invite to someone else's server. sis.fm, the developer's own instance, is invite-only.
• A Spotify account. Servers that enable it also let you sign in with Last.fm.

On first launch the app asks for your server's address, and you can switch servers at any time from Settings.

What it shows
• Now playing — your current track, live
• History — every play, grouped by listening session
• Rankings — top tracks, albums and artists by play count or listening time, over any range
• Weekly charts — new entries, re-entries, peaks and weeks on chart
• Reports — weekly, monthly and yearly recaps of your top artists, albums and tracks
• Insights — hour-by-day heatmaps, genres, streaks and discovery
• Concerts — log the shows you've seen, with their setlists
• Import and export — bring in your Spotify data download, export everything as JSON or CSV

Built for the way you listen
Switch any ranking between plays and time. Filter by all-time, year, 6 months, 3 months, month, week, day or a custom range. Jump to any track, artist or album for its own detail page and history.

Fast and focused
A dense, monospace dashboard with charts that load instantly, keyboard shortcuts for power users, and a global search to find anything in seconds.

Your data, on your server
Your whole listening history stays in a database you control: no third-party analytics, no selling your taste back to you. You can delete your account and all its data from Settings at any time.

Built for iPhone and iPad
Native system bars that follow the theme, a bottom tab bar for quick access on iPhone and the full sidebar layout on iPad.

sis is an independent project and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Spotify.

REVIEW NOTES
sis is a self-hosted tool, like Jellyfin or Nextcloud: each user runs their own sis server (or is invited to one) and the app is its client. For review, use sis.fm, our own instance, with the demo account above. On first launch the app asks for the address of a SIS server: enter "sis.fm" (or tap "Use sis.fm"). Then tap "Sign in with mier.info": the system browser opens the sign-in page of our identity provider (id.mier.info) — enter the demo credentials above and the browser returns to the app automatically. No Spotify account, code or second factor is needed for the demo account.
Spotify appears as the first sign-in option because it is the data source for regular users: the app exists to show the listening history of the user's own Spotify account, which is why there is no Sign in with Apple (guideline 4.8, client of a specific third-party service). The mier.info option is our own account system.
With the demo account the reviewer can see:
• Dashboard: now playing, today's and this week's totals, top tracks, recent plays
• Rankings: top tracks / albums / artists by plays or listening time over any range
• Charts: weekly charts with peaks, weeks on chart and movement
• Insights: hour × day heatmap, genres, streaks and discovery
• History: every play, grouped by listening session
No external account connection is required beyond signing in with the demo account.
Account deletion (guideline 5.1.1(v)): Settings → Account tab → Delete account. After a confirmation step it permanently erases the account and all its data (listening history, ratings, concerts, collections, playlists, connected services) right away; nothing is deactivated or kept, and no email or website visit is needed. Deleting the demo account works the same way, but the demo credentials will then stop working until we restore the account.
